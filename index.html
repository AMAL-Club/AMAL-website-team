About AMAL Club

Empowering Leaders, Inspiring Change

The Amrita Management and Leadership (AMAL) Club at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Bengaluru, is a dynamic student-led organization dedicated to fostering leadership, management, and communication skills. Through interactive workshops, engaging competitions, and real-world simulations, AMAL empowers students to unlock their full potential and thrive in both personal and professional spheres.

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

The Amrita Management and Leadership (AMAL) Club embodies the university’s ethos by fostering management, leadership, and communication skills among students. In alignment with Amrita’s vision of education for life and education for living, AMAL provides opportunities for students. Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham’s commitment to excellence in education and leadership development fuels the growth of student-driven initiatives like AMAL, making it a transformative platform for young professionals of tomorrow.

Our Events

💡 INNOVATE X

The Innovate X Leadership Workshop is an exclusive collaboration between the AMAL Club and ACE (Amrita Centre for Entrepreneurship), designed to empower students with essential leadership, management, and entrepreneurial skills.

🏛 MOCK PARLIAMENT

Debate. Discuss. Decide

The AMAL Mock Parliament is a flagship event designed to simulate real parliamentary proceedings, giving participants a platform to debate, strategize, and engage in policy-making discussions.

🎤 MASTERING THE MIC

Speak with Confidence. Persuade with Power!

Mastering the Mic is an exclusive public speaking and communication workshop by the AMAL Club, designed to help participants develop confidence and sharpen their oratory skills.
